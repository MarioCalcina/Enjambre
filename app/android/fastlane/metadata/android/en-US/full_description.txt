Enjambre is a torrent client with a built-in player: watch videos while they download.

Features:
- Play video and audio files while they download, with subtitles and audio track selection
- Pick which files of a torrent to download
- Organize torrents with labels, search, sort and filter them
- Share torrents as magnet links
- Speed limits and a limit on active downloads
- Keeps downloading in the background, and notifies when a download completes
- Light and dark themes
- Built on Transmission for low CPU and memory usage
- Also available on Windows, Linux and macOS
- Open source software
